=== TITLE ===
ProteoPredict: Tri-Modal Deep Network Fusion for High-Resolution Protein Function Prediction Across Low 3-mer Feature-Space Similarity Regimes

=== ABSTRACT ===
Computational prediction of Gene Ontology (GO) terms from primary amino acid sequences is vital for functional genomics and drug discovery. Traditional homology transfer methods suffer marked performance degradation in low sequence similarity regimes, while monolithic protein language models dilute localized catalytic linear motifs and surface biophysical properties. We present ProteoPredict, a tri-modal multi-branch deep architecture integrating three complementary representations across 2,250 dimensions: Meta AI ESM-2 (320D) evolutionary embeddings, alignment-free 3-mer TF-IDF (1,500D) motifs, and biophysical descriptors (430D) encoding charge, hydropathy, and secondary structure. Features route into decoupled sub-ontology heads optimized with multi-label Focal Loss (gamma = 2.0, alpha = 0.35). From 18,338 retrieved human Swiss-Prot proteins (18,288 unique), 17,624 fully annotated proteins were partitioned into 16,171 disjoint clusters via a 3-mer graph partition heuristic (d_cos <= 0.50, zero cluster overlap across 12,302 train, 2,691 val, and 2,631 test splits). Under CAFA protocols, ProteoPredict achieves a validation-calibrated test Micro-F1 of 0.5031 and Macro-F1 of 0.4997. In this low 3-mer feature-space similarity regime (median 3-mer cosine similarity 0.306), it outperforms homology transfer (F1 = 0.4607) by capturing +35.5% more true annotations (Recall = 0.5917 vs. 0.4366, Micro-F1 gain +0.0424), reaching F1 = 0.6146 in Molecular Function and minimizing CAFA semantic distance to S_min = 19.86 (vs. 22.76). In silico sliding-window Alanine perturbation demonstrates a 1.31-fold functional sensitivity enrichment at verified catalytic residues within the EGFR kinase domain (Mann-Whitney U = 1168.5, logged tie-corrected p = 0.0091 < 0.01; unadjusted normal approximation p = 0.017). Source code and benchmark splits are available at https://github.com/nikhildehariya/proteopredict.
